Before Your Shoot

Get the Home Camera Ready.

A few minutes of prep makes the biggest difference in how a listing looks online. Send this page to your seller before shoot day.

Throughout the Home

  • Turn on every light, including lamps and under-cabinet lights
  • Open all blinds and curtains
  • Replace any burnt-out bulbs
  • Turn off ceiling fans and TVs
  • Close toilet lids and remove bath mats

Kitchen & Baths

  • Clear counters down to one or two decor pieces
  • Put away dish soap, sponges, towels, and toiletries
  • Hide trash cans and pet bowls
  • Take magnets and papers off the fridge

Bedrooms & Living Spaces

  • Make the beds and straighten pillows
  • Put away laundry, toys, cords, and chargers
  • Remove personal photos and paperwork if you prefer they stay private
  • Keep doors open so rooms read as open space

Outside & Shoot Day

  • Move cars out of the driveway and off the street in front
  • Put away hoses, trash bins, and yard tools
  • Plan for pets to be out of the home or crated
  • Make sure we have access: lockbox code or someone on site
